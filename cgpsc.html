About CGPSC

What is CGPSC?

Chhattisgarh Public Service Commission conducts examinations for recruitment to various State Services. It's one of the toughest exams in India requiring dedication and proper guidance.

Exam Stages

Prelims: Objective type questions
Mains: Descriptive paper
Interview: Personal assessment

Why Prepare Here?

Comprehensive resources, updated materials, expert tips, and community support to help you crack the CGPSC exam with flying colors.

Preparation Tips & Strategies

01

Create a Study Schedule

Dedicate 6-8 hours daily for focused study. Divide topics into manageable chunks and maintain consistency.

02

Focus on NCERT

NCERT books are the foundation. Read them thoroughly and make short notes for revision.

03

Solve Previous Papers

Practice last 10 years papers to understand the question pattern and time management.

04

Take Mock Tests

Appear for mock tests regularly to evaluate your preparation and identify weak areas.

05

Read Daily News

Stay updated with current affairs through newspapers and news websites. Focus on CG-specific news.

06

Revise Regularly

Revision is key to success. Use flashcards and short notes for quick revision before exam.
